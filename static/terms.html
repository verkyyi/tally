Sources

Tally records only what a source itself reported: a company's announcement or page, a filing, a price list, a registry's own count, or a news report of an event. A value is recorded only when it can be quoted from the page, and only when the page is reporting a fact, not the publisher's own estimate. Tally does not take figures from commercial company databases, estimate publishers or sites that restate them. Its reader identifies itself as tally-catalog, follows each site's robots.txt, and does not sign in anywhere.

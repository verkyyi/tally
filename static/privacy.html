Tally

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Tally (tallycatalog.com) is a dashboard of public numbers about companies. This page says what Tally collects about you and what it does with it.

What we collect

If you only browse: nothing about you is stored. Our web server keeps standard request logs (IP address, time, page) for security and debugging.

If you ask Tally to research something: the words of your request are published with its result. An email address is optional; if you give one, it is used once, to tell you the research is finished, and is then erased.

If you sign in with Google: we receive your email address, your name and your profile picture from Google, and store them with your account. We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and we never see your Google password.

What you do in Tally: the questions you ask, the metrics you add to your board and the ones you create.

If you pay for Pro: payment is handled by Stripe. Tally never sees or stores your card number. We store a Stripe customer ID and whether your plan is active.

We set one cookie: a sign-in session cookie. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. We keep a daily count of page views and of distinct visitors, per page and per referring site; no IP address, cookie or other detail about you is stored.

What is shared

Tally's catalog is shared by design: a metric you create (its question and its public value) is visible to everyone. Your name and email are not shown next to it; other people see only how many accounts track a metric.

To answer a question, its text is sent to our providers: Telem AI (web search and page reading) and Anthropic (language model). Do not put personal or confidential information in a question.

We do not sell your data.

Keeping and deleting

Account data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and the data tied to it, open an issue at github.com/verkyyi/tally or write to the contact shown on the Stripe receipt. Metrics you created stay in the shared catalog without any link to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.

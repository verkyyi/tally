# Skill: valuation
Read the company's most recently reported valuation.
- Use a valuation the text states as a number, with the event it comes from (a funding round, a tender offer) and its date in the quote.
- Do not compute a valuation from other numbers, and do not use total funding, revenue or a target the company is "seeking".
- When both pre-money and post-money are given, use post-money and say so in the quote.
- If the pages give no valuation figure, return null.

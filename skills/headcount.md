# Skill: headcount
Read how many people work at the company.
- Preserve exact counts, approximations, bounds and ranges as distinct evidence. Never turn "fewer than 120", "100+" or "about 300" into an exact scalar. Name the source and its date in the quote.
- LinkedIn associated members are a different population from employees; do not substitute one for the other.
- A dated article states headcount at that time, not today. Compare newer official sources before choosing evidence.
- Prefer the company's own statement (about page, careers page, press release) over a directory estimate.
- A range such as "201-500 employees" is not one number: return null unless the question asks for the range.
- Never count faces on a team page or infer headcount from open roles.

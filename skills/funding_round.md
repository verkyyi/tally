# Skill: latest funding round
Read the size of the company's most recent funding round.
- Use the newest round the pages report, by announcement date. Name the round and its date in the quote ("Series B, $60M, March 2026").
- The value is the amount raised in that round only, not the company's total funding and not its valuation.
- Prefer the company's own announcement or a named news report over a database summary when they disagree, and prefer the more recent page.
- A round that is only rumoured or "in talks" is not a round. If no closed round is stated, return null.

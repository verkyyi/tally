# Skill: new investments by an investor
List new investments the firm announced or was reported to lead or join during the period in the question.
- One entry per company funded, keyed by the URL of the announcement. Follow-on rounds in an existing portfolio company count as an entry only if the question asks for all investments.
- Title each entry "Firm invests in Company (round)". Give the announcement date when stated.
- Use the firm's own announcements, portfolio news page, or named news reports. Leave out rumours and anything outside the period.

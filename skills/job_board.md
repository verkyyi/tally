# Skill: job board
List the company's open roles that match the function in the question (for example engineering).
- One entry per posting, keyed by the posting's own URL on the job board. Never use the board's index page as an entry.
- Include a role only when its title or team clearly belongs to the function. Leave out design, sales, support and recruiting roles when the question asks for engineering.
- The same role listed for two locations is one entry if it has one URL, two if it has two.
- Leave out closed or archived postings.

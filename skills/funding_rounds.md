# Skill: funding rounds in a sector
List funding rounds announced by startups in the sector during the period in the question.
- One entry per round, keyed by the URL of its announcement or news report. The same round reported by two sites is one entry: keep the more specific URL.
- Title each entry "Company raises $X Series Y". The amount is the round size in USD as a plain number, only when the text states it; otherwise null.
- Include a round only when its announcement date is inside the period and the company clearly belongs to the sector.
- Leave out rumours, rounds "in talks", acquisitions, debt facilities, grants and fund closes by investors.
- Leave out any single amount that the text itself presents as unconfirmed.
